# Why Detection Engineering

Detection engineering means writing, testing and tuning the rules that catch attackers. It is a growing field that sits between SOC analysis and security engineering.

## Evidence
- SANS / Anvilogic 2025 survey: 80% of organizations (85% of large enterprises) are actively investing in detection engineering; 60% have dedicated teams.
- TryHackMe 2026: security engineering and security analysis named by 24% and 23% of hiring managers as priority hiring needs.
- CyberDesserts 2026 career reports: detection engineering listed as one of the fastest-growing role titles.
- Job market trackers show major companies (e.g. CrowdStrike, Bank of America) hiring detection engineers in 2025–2026.

## How this project fits
SOC analyst roles are the entry point; detection engineering is a common next step. This lab builds both: triaging alerts and writing rules from real attack data.
